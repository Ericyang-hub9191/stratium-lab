- **Experimenting** — Being tested; not yet validated
- **Validated** — Supported by credible evidence and approved
- **Rejected** — Considered and intentionally declined
- **Superseded** — Replaced by a later decision

## Initial Record

## DEC-001 — Replace the generic “Duolingo for AI” concept

- **Date:** 2026-08-04
- **Status:** Experimenting
- **Owner:** Eric
- **Area:** Product
- **Constitution sections affected:** 3–10
- **Supersedes:** Previous broad Stratium Lab concept
- **Superseded by:** None

### Decision

Stratium Lab will no longer be developed as a completely general, gamified “Duolingo for AI.” The new direction is an AI workflow and proof-of-skill learning system focused on reliable, job-relevant outcomes.

### Problem or opportunity

The previous product was too broad, visually generic, shallow in content, weakly differentiated, and unlikely to create sufficient willingness to pay.

### Evidence

Initial market research indicates that general AI information, basic prompting, tool news, and introductory explanations are widely available for free. Stronger paid value appears around role-specific workflows, guided projects, feedback, verification, and proof of competence.

### Assumptions

The exact initial segment, first workflow, delivery method, and price remain unvalidated.

### Alternatives considered

- Rebuild the previous general learning platform with better design
- Create an AI-news or tool-comparison site
- Build a broad AI course marketplace

These were rejected as starting points because they do not solve the underlying differentiation and payment problem.

### Expected consequence

Product research and validation will precede full application development. The first product will be narrower and centered on one measurable workflow outcome.

### Risks

- The selected audience may still have insufficient willingness to pay.
- Human feedback may create high delivery cost.
- The first workflow may be too broad or insufficiently urgent.

### Success measure

Repeated pain in interviews, paid founding commitments, strong completion, usable learner artifacts, and evidence of referral or repeat demand.

### Review date

After the first problem-interview and paid-pilot cycle.

### Approval

Approved by Eric: Yes
